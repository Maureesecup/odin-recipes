Baked Mac & Cheese

a classic comfort food made with cooked macaroni pasta mixed into a rich cheese sauce, transferred to a baking dish.

Uses a creamy cheese sauce, often a béchamel or roux made from butter, flour, and milk—combined with generous amounts of melted cheese like sharp cheddar,
frequently finished off with a crispy or golden layer, such as buttered breadcrumbs, panko, or extra shredded cheese.

Ingredients>

  • 1lb elbow macaroni(or short pasta)
  • 6tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 4 cups freshly shredded cheese
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp onion powder
  • 1/4 tsp mustard powder
  • 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs for topping(optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat and Boil: Heat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
    Boil the macaroni in a large pot of salted water for 2 minutes less than the package directions so it stays firm.
    Drain and toss with a small splash of butter.
  2. Make Roux: Melt 6 tablespoons of butter in a large saucepan over medium heat.
    then add the flour and cook and stir constantly for 1 minute.
  3. Add the Dairy: Slowly pour in the warm milk and heavy cream, whisk vigorously to prevent lumps.
  4. Season and Thicken: Add the salt, pepper, garlic powder, onion powder, and mustard powder,
    then let simmer for 3 to 5 minutes until the sauce has become thick enough to coat the back of a spoon.
  5. Melt the Cheese: Remove the pan from the heat.
    Stir in 3 cups of your shredded cheese until the sauce is smooth and melted.
  6. Combine: Add the cooked macaroni to the cheese sauce and stir well to coat every piece.
  7. Assemble and Bake: Pour the mixture into a greased 9x13-inch baking dish.
    Top with the remaining 1 cup of cheese and the panko breadcrumbs.
  8. Bake: Bake uncovered at 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes until it's bubbly and golden brown on top.
    You can also broil for the final 2 minutes if you want some extra crispiness.
